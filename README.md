I'm Prashant Kumar, Founder & CEO of Bibha AI Labs, an AI-powered workforce platform based in Bengaluru with a US entity, Bibha AI Inc., in California. I started Bibha in mid-2024 to build the infrastructure for a workforce where humans and AI agents work side by side. We run three core products: TalentFlow, which automates recruitment end to end; AgentsHub, our digital staff factory for building and deploying AI agents; and PeachDesk, a self-hosted, bring-your-own-key voice AI platform.

I've spent more than a decade as a hands-on engineer and R&D leader across some very different domains: retail tech at Target, industrial IoT and SCADA systems, autonomous vehicles at Harman (including Daimler truck testing), and fleet management. Most recently I was Head of Engineering at Azuga, through its acquisition by Bridgestone. I started working with LLMs in early 2019, well before they were mainstream, and that early conviction is what eventually led me to found Bibha.

I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science (2014), an MBA in Global Business from the University of Western Australia, and an Executive Management Development Programme in Business Analytics from the Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode.
