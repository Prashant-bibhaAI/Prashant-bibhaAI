## 👋 About Me

I'm Prashant Kumar, Founder & CEO of Bibha AI Labs, an AI-powered workforce platform based in Bengaluru with a US entity, Bibha AI Inc., in California. I started Bibha in mid-2024 to build the infrastructure for a workforce where humans and AI agents work side by side.<br/><br/>

We run three core products:

- **TalentFlow** — automates recruitment end to end
- **AgentsHub** — our digital staff factory for building and deploying AI agents
- **PeachDesk** — a self-hosted, bring-your-own-key voice AI platform

---

## 🛠️ Background

I've spent more than a decade as a hands-on engineer and R&D leader across some very different domains:

- Retail tech at **Target**
- Industrial IoT and SCADA systems
- Autonomous vehicles at **Harman**, including Daimler truck testing
- Fleet management

Most recently I was Head of Engineering at **Azuga**, through its acquisition by Bridgestone. I started working with LLMs in early 2019, well before they were mainstream, and that early conviction is what eventually led me to found Bibha.
